Our source — Kathmandu grading line

Our stock moves on harvest rhythms, not trend cycles. Bodhi and rudraksha are collected from the Terai lowlands and lower Himalayan foothills during short seasonal windows when mature seeds dry naturally and can be sorted without forced heat. Collectors bring sacks into Kathmandu where our grading table checks diameter bands, face definition, crack rejection, and drilling accuracy lot by lot. Each kilogram is hand-sieved twice, then matched for color consistency so makers can string from one lot without jarring shifts. We work direct with harvest families and pay against verified weight, then document each outbound batch by seed type, millimeter range, and grade outcome. The result is simple: a graded kilogram that behaves predictably at the bench, whether you buy 0.5kg for boutique runs or 25kg for wholesale inventory.

Shipping and customs

Dispatch is from Kathmandu by DHL or FedEx, packed by lot and weighed before seal. Typical transit is 7–14 days depending on customs lane and destination city. Tracking is issued on handoff day. Import duty, VAT, and customs brokerage are paid by the buyer in the receiving country. For 25kg+ orders, request palletized freight and lane-specific quotes.
